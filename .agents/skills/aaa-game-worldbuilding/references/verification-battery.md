# Verification Battery

After a major world pass, capture the viewport and inspect these categories:

1. Composition and player route.
2. Terrain geology and silhouette.
3. Vegetation variety and density.
4. Near-field ground and surface detail.
5. Lighting, shadow color, and subject readability.
6. Atmosphere, cloud structure, and distance.
7. Water, shore transitions, and crossings.
8. Motion and visual noise.
9. Gameplay safety, spawn, traversal, and orientation.
10. Performance: frame time when available, draw/triangle/instance counts,
    pass costs, and quality-preset suitability.

Write the three largest deltas, make the highest-impact corrections, then
capture again. Do not declare AAA-style quality from configuration alone.
