# Gameplay and Exploration

## Player-first environment work

Visual ambition does not replace playable space. Add a valid player spawn,
continuous traversal, readable collision, sensible jump and clearance, clear
routes, orientation cues, and intentional rest, combat, or exploration rhythm.

## Open-world RPG region

Build multiple readable biomes, a major landmark, a settlement or authored
structure, primary and secondary routes, water crossings, a spawn, exploration
points, and distant navigation cues. The procedural foundation must be paired
with hand-authored choices.

## Survival world

Place resource zones, water access, shelter, navigable terrain, distinct
biomes, weather, readable danger zones, and a performance level suitable for
gameplay rather than a one-frame capture.

## Interiors

When the task is an interior, do not create a procedural open world just
because this skill is active. Use connected editable mesh shells, correct
scale, material variation, lighting hierarchy, decals, props, gameplay hooks,
paths, and visual verification while preserving the basic traversal route.
