# Composition and Art Direction

## Reference-delta loop

When a user provides an image, first identify camera height and lens,
foreground, main subject, horizon, terrain silhouette, vegetation density,
lighting direction, atmospheric depth, palette, and water placement. Create or
choose a matching bookmark, build large forms first, capture the viewport, list
the meaningful visual differences, fix the top three, and capture again.

Do not merely add similarly colored objects. Match composition, depth, scale,
silhouette, light, and the hierarchy of attention.

## Hierarchy

Give the player a foreground frame, a midground story subject, and a distant
orientation landmark. Make water, roads, ridgelines, and vegetation bands lead
the eye and clarify traversal. Preserve warm/cool separation and local contrast
around the subject.

## Specialized looks

- Forest ravine: carved channel, layered canopy, understory, debris, wet
  margins, moss, readable shafts, streambed detail, and a walkable route.
- Alpine vista: massif silhouette, altitude/slope snow, valley canopy, clouds
  among peaks, foreground frame, atmospheric depth, and long-distance fidelity.
- Cinematic showcase: explicit bookmark, controlled lens/composition, filmic
  lighting, color script, restrained motion, suitable high or ultra preset,
  screenshot comparison, and optional flythrough.
